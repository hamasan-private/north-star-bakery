Maplewood, since 2009

Bread out of the oven before the sun is up

Welcome to North Star Bakery, a two-oven bakery on Alder Street. We bake through the night so the first loaves are still warm when we unlock the door at 6:30. Most of our customers are on their way to the bus stop or dropping kids at school, and we like being part of that morning.

What we're known for

People come to us for three things, and we have stopped pretending otherwise.

Country sourdough

Our starter is named Dot and has been going since 2009, so every loaf comes from the same flour and water Ruth mixed the week we opened.

Saturday cinnamon rolls

We make about sixty, they are gone by ten, and people have strong opinions about whether they need more cinnamon. They do.

Cakes for celebrations

Birthdays, weddings, and office retirements. Two days' notice for a round cake, a week for anything tiered.

We also do a lot of pre-orders. If you need twelve sandwich rolls for an office lunch or a sheet cake for a retirement party, tell us two days ahead and we will have it boxed and waiting with your name on it.

Featured this week

Honey oat sandwich loaf

A soft loaf with rolled oats on top and a little honey in the dough. It is the one bread we make that holds up to a peanut butter sandwich on day three, which is why the commuters buy two at a time.

$8 a loaf, or $21 for three.
