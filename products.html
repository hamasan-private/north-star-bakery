What we bake

Our menu is short on purpose. We would rather make eleven things well than forty things badly, so what is on this page is close to everything that comes out of the ovens in a normal week. Anything marked "Saturday only" is gone by ten, so come early or pre-order it.

Breads

All of our bread is naturally leavened except the sandwich loaf, which uses a small amount of yeast so it stays soft. Loaves come out at 6:30 a.m. and again around 2:00 p.m.

  • Country sourdough, $7 — the one Dot makes. Dark crust, open crumb, good with butter and nothing else.
  • Honey oat sandwich loaf, $8 — soft, and sliced on request.
  • Seeded rye, $9 — caraway and sunflower, dense enough for an open-faced sandwich.
  • Baguette, $4.50 — Saturdays only.

Pastries

Everything in the case is mixed the afternoon before and baked between 4:00 and 6:00 a.m. If a tray is empty by noon, it usually stays empty.

  • Croissants and almond croissants, $4 to $5.50
  • Cinnamon rolls, $4.50 — Saturdays only, and we make about sixty of them.
  • Cookies, $2.50 each or $12 for six — oatmeal, chocolate chip, and raspberry thumbprint.
  • Morning buns and scones, $4 to $4.75

Cakes and celebration orders

We take cake orders for birthdays, weddings, and office parties. Cakes are priced by size, and we need two days' notice for anything round and one week for a tiered cake. We do not do printed photos on cakes, but we can write on them.

  • 6-inch round, $38 — serves 6 to 8
  • 9-inch round, $58 — serves 12 to 16
  • Sheet cake, $85 — serves about 40
  • Tiered cake, from $180 — quote after we talk
